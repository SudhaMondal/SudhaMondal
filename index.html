A little about me

Science with a human pulse.

I'm pursuing B.Tech in Biomedical Engineering at Adamas University. My interests range from artificial heart systems and cardiac biomechanics to cognitive neuroscience, biomaterials and accessible diagnostic technologies.

I enjoy turning ideas into tangible experiments and digital experiences — learning by building, documenting and refining along the way.
