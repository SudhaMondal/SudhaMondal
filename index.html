Profile

Science, systems & human stories.

Biomedical engineering student · interdisciplinary researcher-in-training · builder · communicator.

I am Sudha Mondal, a B.Tech Biomedical Engineering student at Adamas University. I am drawn to ambitious questions at the intersection of biology and engineering: how artificial heart systems can support life, how the brain gives rise to cognition, and how accessible technologies can make scientific tools more useful.

Alongside my academic interests, I build digital projects, explore design and documentation, and value communication, collaboration and service. My work spans technical experiments, creative software, media experience and community involvement.

My approach is interdisciplinary: understand the problem carefully, learn the tools, make something tangible, document it clearly, and keep improving it.

“From artificial hearts to the architecture of thought — engineering tomorrow’s MedTech.”
